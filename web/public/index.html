Festival du Rire

Four days of stand-up, galas and workshops on the Deauville seafront, from the Grand Casino to a beach hut by the lifeguard post.

      The festival

      Laughter, on the boardwalk

      Deauville has entertained its summer crowds since the 1860s: concerts at the casino, promenades on Les Planches, evenings that run late. The Festival du Rire brings that tradition to comedy.

      For four days in June, the town's stages belong to stand-up: solo shows in the theatre, galas at the Grand Casino, late-night lineups on the beach promenade, and workshops for anyone who wants to try.
